Privacy Policy

PrankCam · Effective November 19, 2026

PrankCam is designed so that photos never leave your iPhone unless you choose to share them. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send photos or any other information to us or to any third party.

Camera

PrankCam asks for permission to use the camera. It uses both cameras: the rear camera shows the live viewfinder, and when the shutter is pressed the front camera takes one photo, which shows the person holding the iPhone. The app explains this before it asks for camera access. PrankCam never records video or audio and does not use the microphone. iOS shows its camera-in-use indicator whenever the camera is on, and the camera is turned off while a photo is displayed.

Photos you take

Face detection and the funny effects are processed entirely on your iPhone using Apple's built-in frameworks. The resulting photo is kept only in the app's memory. It is not saved anywhere unless you tap Save or Share, and it is discarded when you tap Try Again or Done or close the app.

Photo library

The first time you tap Save, PrankCam asks for permission to add photos to your library. It only adds the photo you chose to save, and it cannot see or read your existing photos.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the photo is passed to the iOS share sheet and sent only to the app or person you choose. PrankCam itself does not upload it.

Settings stored on your device

Your choices, such as the effect, strength, stamp and haptics, are saved on your iPhone only. They do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
