Frequently asked questions

How does the prank work?

The screen shows the rear camera like a normal camera. When the shutter is pressed, PrankCam takes a photo with the front camera, which captures the person holding the iPhone, applies the effect you chose and shows it right away.

Are my photos uploaded or saved automatically?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. A prank photo stays in memory until you tap Save or Share, and Try Again or Done discards it.

How do I change the effect or the stamp?

Pick an effect on the home screen before you start, or tap a different effect on the photo after it's taken. Tap Stamp at the top of the photo to choose a message, a random one, or no stamp. You can also change both in Settings.

The camera screen says "Camera access is off".

Open the iPhone Settings app › PrankCam and turn on Camera.

Save says PrankCam isn't allowed to add photos.

Open Settings › PrankCam › Photos and choose "Add Photos Only".

Why is there a short pause when I press the shutter?

Most iPhones run both cameras at once, so the photo is instant. On iPhones that can't, PrankCam briefly switches to the front camera and covers the screen while it does.

Is it OK to prank people?

Keep it friendly: only prank people who'll enjoy it, always show them the photo, and delete it if they ask.
